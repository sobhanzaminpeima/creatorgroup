## Sources and freshness
Country intelligence lives in lib/country-model.ts and lib/country-seeds.ts; CMS overrides live in country_intelligence. Each substantive block carries a title, URL, source type, jurisdiction, verified date, modification date and verifier. `needs-verification` and `planning` blocks are labelled. Verified blocks older than 90 days show a freshness warning; an assistant never upgrades them to current rules. Information is an overview, not an individual visa/admission decision. Republic of Cyprus is explicitly distinguished from northern Cyprus. Current catalogue universities are in Türkiye; other countries do not show fictional universities.

Country assistant retrieves structured platform knowledge. A generative AI provider is not configured. Google/Apple OAuth and live flight/hotel purchases need genuine provider credentials; unavailable integrations are not displayed as functioning buttons or prices. Immigration guidance is edited in the existing admin panel's Countries & Official Sources tab, with explicit source-review confirmation and audit logging.
